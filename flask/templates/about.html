Customer Retention System

Smart analytics platform to predict churn and retain valuable customers

Overview

Customer Retention System is a complete web-based solution built to help businesses identify customers at risk of leaving and take timely action. It combines customer management, machine learning predictions, and powerful reporting tools into one simple platform.

Mission

Reduce customer churn through accurate insights, early risk detection, and efficient retention strategies — all in a secure and easy-to-use system.

Key Capabilities
Customer Management Add, edit, search and organize customer records
CSV Import Bulk upload customer data with one click
Churn Prediction ML-powered risk scoring for every customer
Retention Alerts Automatic high-risk customer notifications
Analytics Dashboard Visual insights into churn and retention
Reports Export PDF & CSV reports for management
Technology Stack
Frontend HTML5 · CSS3 · Bootstrap 5 · JavaScript
Backend Python Flask
Database MySQL
Machine Learning Scikit-learn
